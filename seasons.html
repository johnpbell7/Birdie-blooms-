through the seasons

The flowers change, so do the colours.

Birdie Blooms follows the British growing year. The website does too: its colours shift four times a year to match what’s in the buckets, all by itself.

how it works

Switches by date, no fuss.

Winter runs December to February, spring March to May, summer June to August and autumn September to November. On the first day of each new season the site picks up its bloom colour, pastel tint, accent, homepage photo and tab icon.

Previewing another season only lasts until you close the tab.

Seasonal letters.

What’s coming into season, first dibs on peony week and December wreath workshops, and the odd Birdie Card bonus. Four a year, never more.
