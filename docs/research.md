# Birdie Blooms — research: high-end yet approachable independent florists

*Compiled October 2026. Fonts and prices were read from each site's own HTML, CSS and product data (Shopify `products.json`, Squarespace JSON), so they reflect what the sites actually ship. Visual notes are inferred from themes, markup and copy.*

## 2. Patterns shared by the best of them

1. **"Florist's choice" with a seasonal disclaimer** on every product — *"flowers may differ… always in similar colours and feel."*
2. **Named bouquets and a characterful 3–5 step size ladder**, roughly doubling from smallest to largest, often with a vase add-on.
3. **Delivery facts up front** — cut-off and fee in the announcement bar, repeated in the FAQ.
4. **Weddings split two ways** — a priced "ready-to-wear" list for small weddings, plus bespoke work with a stated minimum and a clear consult → proposal → on-the-day process.
5. **The founder is named and photographed**, with a short first-person quote.
6. **Specific sustainability claims** — no floral foam, plastic-free wrapping, % British-grown — and honesty about compromises.
7. **Trust** — press, client names, credited photographers, Instagram, newsletter.
8. **Restrained motion** — fade-ups, a marquee, light parallax. Only Studio Mondine goes further with GSAP.
9. **Type** — Garamond-family serif + Futura-like geometric sans is the dominant pairing.

## 3. UK price ladder (2025/26)

London studios sit roughly 25–40% above regional ones.

**Wedding norms:** book 6–12 months ahead · 25–30% non-refundable deposit · balance 4–6 weeks before · UK average flower spend ≈ £1,130–1,190 (Bridebook).

## 4. Forms

**Wedding enquiry** (most-asked first): names · email · phone (optional) · date (allow "not set yet") · ceremony & reception venue · guest count · **budget band** · what you need (checkboxes) · colours or "three words for the feel" · flowers you love / dislike · Pinterest link · planner/photographer · how you heard · message · consent. Keep the "don't worry if you don't have everything yet" tone.

**General contact:** name · email · phone · enquiry type (Order / Wedding / Event / Sympathy / Subscription / Other) · message.

---

## 5. Voice

**Use:** seasonal, hand-tied, gathered, generous, garden-led, loose, unfussy, "florist's choice", "whatever's best this week", "never the same twice", "British-grown when the season allows", "a small studio", "let's talk flowers", "tell me about your day", "made by hand, delivered by me". Name real stems. Write in the first person.

**Avoid:** saying "luxury" or "high-end" outright; "stunning", "exquisite", "elevate", "curated" and "bespoke" in every line; "dream wedding"; flower puns; "wow factor"; ALL CAPS; exclamation marks; vague "eco-friendly" (say "no floral foam, ever" and "plastic-free wrapping" instead).

---

## 6. Fonts (Google Fonts)

1. **Cormorant Garamond + Jost** — the free version of the category's Garamond + Futura pairing. Classic, bridal; very common among florists.
2. **Fraunces (SOFT axis) + Figtree** — the warmest, most approachable option.
3. **Instrument Serif + Instrument Sans** — modern editorial, couture; close to Studio Mondine; superb with GSAP line reveals.

## 7. How this shaped the Birdie Blooms build

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| **Della Respira + EB Garamond + Josefin Sans** | Revised once her real logo was found. Her wordmark is a high-contrast Art Nouveau serif and her tagline is set in Josefin Sans. Della Respira echoes the wordmark without copying it, EB Garamond gives warm, bookish reading text ("a little bit of yesterday"), and Josefin Sans carries labels and buttons exactly as in her logo. The first pass (Instrument Serif + Figtree) read as generic and AI-made, so it was dropped. |
| **White, warm ink, one dusty-rose accent** | "White and clean", with warm off-black and a paper-cream for alternate sections so it never feels clinical. |
| **Named bouquets with Classic / Generous / Abundant sizes** | Mirrors the S&V / Wild at Heart ladders; prices pitched at the upper end of regional indie. |
| **Seasonal disclaimer, cut-off and fee everywhere it matters** | Announcement bar, shop strip, basket and FAQ. |
| **Weddings: three packages + à la carte price guide + stated minimum** | Grace & Thorn's "ready-to-wear" clarity combined with Queen of the Meadow's bespoke process. |
| **First-person founder story on home and About** | Floret / Swallows & Damsons / Queen of the Meadow. |
| **Specific sustainability claims, no vague "eco"** | S&V pledge. |
| **GSAP motion kept refined** | Masked line reveals, clip-path image wipes, a marquee, light parallax, one pinned gallery, page fades. All switched off for reduced-motion users. |
| **No invented reviews or press** | Testimonials and press logos only work when they're real (and fake reviews are now illegal in the UK under the DMCC Act 2024). The quote carousel uses the florist's own words until real client reviews are added. |
