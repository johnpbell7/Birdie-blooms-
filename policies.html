Delivery, care & policies.

Everything you need to know about getting flowers from my bench to your door, keeping them happy, and how I look after your details.

Delivery & collection Seasonal substitutions Flower care Changes & refunds Wedding bookings Privacy Terms

Delivery & collection

  • Same-day: order by 11am, Tuesday to Saturday, for delivery the same day.
  • Local delivery: £6.50 within 12 miles of the studio, free on orders over £75. Deliveries usually arrive between 11am and 5pm.
  • Further afield: often possible; ask and I’ll quote.
  • Dried flowers: posted anywhere in mainland UK, tracked, for £5.95.
  • Collection: free from the studio, by appointment.
  • If nobody’s in, I’ll leave flowers somewhere safe and shaded, or with a neighbour, and text you a photo.

Seasonal substitutions

Every bunch is made by hand with the best flowers of the week, so yours may differ from the photograph. If a particular stem isn’t at its best, I’ll swap it for something of equal or greater value in the same colours and feel. If a specific flower really matters, tell me in your order notes and I’ll check before I make it.

Flower care

  • Keep your bouquet in its water pouch until you’re ready to arrange it.
  • Re-cut each stem at an angle, about 2cm up, with clean, sharp scissors.
  • Use a clean vase and fresh, cool water with the flower food provided.
  • Remove any leaves that sit below the water line.
  • Change the water every two days and give the stems a fresh trim.
  • Keep flowers away from radiators, full sun, draughts and fruit bowls (ripening fruit makes flowers fade faster).
  • Dried flowers need no water: keep them out of direct sun and damp, and give them a gentle shake to dust.

Changes & refunds

Because flowers are perishable and made to order, they can’t be returned. You can change or cancel an order free of charge up to 24 hours before delivery. If anything arrives less than lovely, please send a photo within 24 hours and I’ll put it right with a replacement or a refund.

Subscriptions can be paused, skipped or cancelled at any time before the Friday ahead of your next delivery.

Wedding bookings

  • A 25% non-refundable deposit secures your date.
  • Final numbers are confirmed six weeks before, and the balance is due four weeks before the wedding.
  • Vases, urns and frames are on loan and collected at clear-down.
  • Flowers are seasonal, so like-for-like substitutions may be needed. I’ll always talk you through them.

Privacy

When you send an order, enquiry or newsletter sign-up, I use your details only to reply, deliver your flowers and, if you’ve asked, send seasonal emails. I never sell or share them, except with the services needed to run the studio (email, payments and the form provider). You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing hello@birdieblooms.co.uk.

This site doesn’t use tracking or advertising cookies. Your basket is remembered on your own device only.

Terms

Orders are confirmed once payment has been received. Prices include VAT where applicable and may change with the season. Birdie Blooms is a trading name; full business details are available on request.
