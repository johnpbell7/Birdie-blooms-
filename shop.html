How it works

From my bench to their door.

i.

Choose a style & size

Pick a bunch and a size, add a card message, and choose delivery or collection. No payment is taken until I’ve confirmed.

Made that morning

I make it the morning it leaves, with the freshest stems in the studio. No floral foam, no plastic, just water, paper and string.

iii.

Hand-delivered by me

Delivered within 12 miles of the studio, usually between 11am and 5pm. I’ll text you a photo when they’re on the doorstep.
