Meet the florist

Hello, I’m Hannah.

Birdie Blooms began on my kitchen table with a bucket of market flowers and a habit of giving them away.

Today every order is still made by me, in my studio in Ravenstone and Kibworth. I buy fresh each week, choose British-grown stems whenever they’re in season, and you’ll talk to me from your first message to the moment your flowers arrive.
