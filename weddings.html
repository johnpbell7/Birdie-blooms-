The approach

Like they were gathered that morning.

I love flowers that move: stems that lean, petals that drop, colours that change with the light. Nothing stiff, nothing sprayed, nothing in foam.

I take a small number of weddings each year so every one gets my full attention. I’ll design around your venue and your season, use British-grown flowers whenever the season allows, and make sure every arrangement that can be moved is moved, from ceremony to reception.

How it works

Four steps, no stress.

01

Enquire

Tell me your date, your venue and a little about you both. I’ll check availability and reply within one working day, with a rough idea of cost.

02

Consultation

A relaxed chat at the studio, at your venue or on a video call. We’ll talk colours, flowers, the feel of the day and your budget, honestly. It’s free.

03

Proposal

You’ll get a mood board, the flowers I’d use for your month and an itemised quote. A 25% deposit secures your date; the balance is due four weeks before.

04

On the day

I deliver, set up and style everything, pin on buttonholes if you’d like, then come back to clear down, so you never have to think about vases.

Good questions

Things couples usually ask.

Anything else? Ask me directly.

How far ahead should I book?
Six to twelve months is ideal, especially for Saturdays between May and September. Elopements and smaller weddings can often be done with a few weeks' notice, so it’s always worth asking.
Is there a minimum spend?
Full-service weddings start from £1,500. For smaller days, the Elopement collection starts at £495 and is delivered to your venue.
Do you use floral foam?
Never. Everything is built with reusable chicken wire, moss, pin frogs and water tubes. It’s better for the planet and the flowers drink better too.
Can I choose specific flowers?
Absolutely. I’ll steer you towards what’s at its best in your month, so your flowers look and last their best, but favourites and meaningful stems are always welcome.
Do you travel?
I regularly work within 40 miles of Harpenden. Further afield is lovely too, with travel and accommodation added at cost.
Can the ceremony flowers move to the reception?
Yes, and I design for it. Urns, meadows and aisle flowers can be moved to frame the top table or the bar, so nothing is used only once.
