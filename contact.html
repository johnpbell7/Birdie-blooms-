Questions

Good to know.

Wedding questions are answered on the weddings page.

Where do you deliver?
Hand-delivered by me within 12 miles of the studio in Harpenden, for £6.50, or free on orders over £75. Further afield is often possible, so just ask. Dried flowers can be posted anywhere in mainland UK.
When do I need to order by?
Order by 11am for same-day delivery, Tuesday to Saturday. Deliveries usually arrive between 11am and 5pm. For a particular day, order as early as you like and choose the date at checkout.
Can I collect instead?
Of course, and it’s free. Collections are from the studio by appointment; choose collection at checkout and I’ll confirm a time.
Can I choose specific flowers?
Yes. Bouquets are florist’s choice by default, which gets you the best of the week, but tell me colours, favourites or a flower that means something and I’ll do my best.
How do I pay?
Send your order request and I’ll confirm availability, then send a secure card payment link. Nothing is taken until you’ve approved it.
How do I keep my flowers fresh?
Re-cut the stems at an angle, use a clean vase and fresh water, change it every two days and keep them away from radiators, fruit and full sun. A care card comes with every bunch, and there’s a full guide on the flower care page.
