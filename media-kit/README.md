## Logos

Each logo has its own folder with three formats:

- **`svg/`**: vector, any size. Use for print, signage and anything that scales.
- **`png/`**: transparent background, 2000 px on the longest side. Use over photos or colour.
- **`jpg/`**: on a background, 2400 × 1600 px. Use when you just need an image to share.

## Website visuals

- **`website-visuals/logos-on-the-website/`:** each logo on the same neutral home page, desktop and phone.
- **`website-visuals/homepages/`:** the two home pages designed around a logo (02 Groovy Bloom and 03 The Dove), desktop and phone, as the first screen and as the full page.

The PNGs are saved with an exact colour palette, so they are pixel-for-pixel identical to the full-colour originals at a third of the size.
